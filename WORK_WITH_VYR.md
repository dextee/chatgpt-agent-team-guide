## What would your team like to stop repeating?

An agent team can be useful when a recurring task needs several kinds of work: reading a request, checking approved information, preparing an action and handling exceptions. The starting point is the business process and the result you need.

Examples to explore include preparing invoice checks, assembling prospect research, routing support requests and answering routine booking questions. Each needs its own data access, controls and acceptance criteria.

## What happens next

1. **Check the fit.** Confirm the process, decision maker, expected involvement, budget and timing.
2. **Agree the scope.** Document inputs, integrations, human approvals, exceptions, deliverables and commercial terms.
3. **Build and evaluate.** Test representative cases and failure handling against an agreed baseline.
4. **Release and measure.** Confirm ownership and ongoing support; track the result after launch.

A more capable model alone does not establish a useful business outcome. The relevant evidence is whether the completed workflow meets its acceptance criteria at an acceptable total cost.
